## Next Art Direction Before Final Art Lock

- Add more painterly candle haze, antique glass, velvet, paper grain, and silk folds.
- Add more human presence through hands, posture, silhouettes, or partial portraits without becoming celebrity-like.
- Keep rose as an accent, not a full pink wash.
- Make each Major Arcana composition more unique before scaling repeated motifs.
- Preserve the non-deterministic reading posture: the deck reveals feelings and choices, not fixed outcomes.
