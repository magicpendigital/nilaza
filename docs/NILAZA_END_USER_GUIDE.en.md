# Nilaza user guide

> Open Reflection 1.0.0 · Beta preview 0.1.0 · 2026-09-11. Prepared, not released.

## Getting started with Nilaza

### What is Nilaza?

Nilaza is an AI companion for self-discovery and reflection. It combines conversation, AI guides, tarot, astrology, and journaling. It is not a fortune-telling service or professional advice.

### First setup

1. On the welcome screen, choose your language, then **Create account** or **Log in**. Confirm your email if prompted before continuing.
2. After your first sign-in, choose 1–3 interests or **Skip for now**. Nilaza uses your account name without asking again. If saving fails, retry on this screen.
3. On **Home**, choose **Start a moment of reflection** to open Today Pulse. After opening it that day, the button becomes **Revisit your reflection**.
4. Choose **Chat** or **Open Journal** for your next step. The five navigation tabs remain **Home**, **Explore**, **Chat**, **Journal**, and **Profile**.

You can change interests, response tone, and language in **Profile**. New accounts start with a gentle tone. Birth details are optional in your profile/astrology entry. Returning users who completed setup do not repeat it.

### Current status

Nilaza is in limited beta. Some sync, Premium, and astrology flows are still being refined; status labels inside the app are the most accurate source for the build you are using.

[Help](https://nilaza.magicpendigital.com/en/help/getting-started)

## Oracle and Today Pulse

### Nilaza Oracle

From **Chat**, open an Oracle conversation when you want to name what is creating tension, separate fact from assumption, or find a small action within your control.

Oracle may suggest continuing with a guide, tarot, or journaling. These are navigation suggestions, not verdicts about the future.

### Today Pulse

The first time you open it each day, you see the card back. Take a moment, then tap the card to reveal your reflection. Closing before tapping does not mark it as viewed. After revealing the card, returning opens the same day's content.

Today Pulse is a daily reflection rhythm. On **Home**, choose **Start a moment of reflection**; after opening it for the first time that day, choose **Revisit your reflection**. Read the prompt, check in with your mood if you wish, and close it to return to Home. It may use your profile and interests for personalization, but it is not a certain prediction.

> A useful question often begins with “What do I know clearly?” rather than forcing the future into a fixed conclusion.

[Help](https://nilaza.magicpendigital.com/en/help/oracle-today-pulse)

## AI guides and Council

### AI guides

Each persona has its own voice, questioning style, and scope. Historical or book personas are AI experiences **inspired by** a figure or work, not the real person, author, publisher, estate, or official representative.

### AI Council

Council helps you explore a topic through complementary perspectives. The first guide opens the round; later guides may build on or respond to an earlier lens before the room offers a light synthesis. You remain the decision-maker; this is not a debate between real people.

Do not share passwords, API keys, payment-card details, identity documents, or information you do not want processed.

[Help](https://nilaza.magicpendigital.com/en/help/guides-council)

## Reflective tarot

### Open a reading

Each draw shows the newly revealed card at the center with your draw count. For a multi-card spread, choose **Draw next card** to continue. Once all cards are revealed, choose **View message** to open the overall interpretation. Tap a revealed card for a closer look.

Choose a deck and spread, enter a question, then draw cards as guided. The app selects the cards; AI interprets only the selected cards and the context you provide.

### Ask better questions

Prefer open questions such as “What do I need to see more clearly?” or “What small step is within my control?”. Do not treat tarot as evidence for medical, legal, or financial decisions.

You can save a useful interpretation to your Journal for later reflection.

[Help](https://nilaza.magicpendigital.com/en/help/tarot)

## Journal, insights, and data

### Journal and insights

Save an insight from a conversation or reading using the save action where available. From **Home → Open Journal** or the **Journal** tab, find and revisit saved items. Standalone notes are not included in this wave. Your Journal is for reflection, not for passwords, medical records, financial information, or identity documents.

### Local storage and sync

Nilaza is moving from a local-first MVP toward protected sync flows. Exact behavior depends on the build. Check **Profile → Privacy & data** before assuming an item has synced or been deleted across every device.

No system should make an absolute safety promise. Safety requires access controls, encrypted transport, backend checks, deletion workflows, and operational monitoring.

[Help](https://nilaza.magicpendigital.com/en/help/journal-data)

## Account, recovery, and notifications

### Account and recovery

Online authentication and password-recovery flows are being tested for the production pilot. If a recovery link expires, request a new one rather than sharing a code or token with anyone else.

### In-app inbox

The **Notifications** inbox is available in the beta build. You can filter unread items, mark one or all as read, open the related content, or dismiss an item. The inbox remains the reliable fallback when push is not ready.

### Push notifications

Push notifications are optional and should use generic copy; tarot questions, chat text, journal content, birth data, and sensitive AI results do not belong on the lock screen.

You can enable or disable each reminder type from **Profile → Notifications**. If you do not grant push permission, the in-app inbox still works.

[Help](https://nilaza.magicpendigital.com/en/help/account-notifications)

## Astrology and Premium: beta status

### Astrology

Your birth profile is optional. Astrology placements must be calculated by a deterministic library or service before AI interpretation; AI must not invent them. The full report flow is still being refined.

### Premium and credits

The Premium screen and credit foundation exist in the MVP for experience testing. Pricing, limits, benefits, payment, and restore behavior become commitments only when shown in the production release and app stores.

Usage always follows the limits displayed in the current release.

[Help](https://nilaza.magicpendigital.com/en/help/astrology-premium)

## Waitlist and beta access

### After signup

The “You're on the list” message means Nilaza recorded your request and selected interest. It is not yet an app account or an immediate access invitation.

For now, Nilaza will use the address you provided to notify you when a suitable beta cohort opens. You do not need to provide a password or payment information to keep your place on the waitlist.

### Email from Nilaza

Beta emails should contain only product updates, introductory content related to your selected interest, and a beta invitation when capacity is available. Once automated email is enabled, every marketing email will include an unsubscribe link.

### Invitations and Premium

Joining the waitlist does not guarantee an invitation date. Pricing, limits, and Premium benefits become commitments only when they appear in the production release and app stores.

Ignore impersonation emails or pages that ask for a password, recovery code, or payment to keep a beta place.

[Help](https://nilaza.magicpendigital.com/en/help/beta-waitlist)
