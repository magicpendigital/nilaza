## Explore without birth information

From **Explore → Astrology**, choose **Explore the 12 signs** or **Mythology world**. The library has 12 symbolic chapters with an interactive zodiac wheel, mythology, and reflection prompts. Birth information is not required to read it.

## Personal charts and Chart Story

Choose **Create a personal chart** if you want to add birth details voluntarily. Placements are calculated on the device before interpretation; AI must not invent them. If your birth time is uncertain, read the displayed limits rather than treating every placement as exact.

Chart Story helps you explore calculated placements. The test build supports saving and reopening local charts. Local storage does not automatically mean a chart has synced to the cloud.

## AI, Premium, and release

Astrology is integrated in the internal test build. AI interpretation depends on your account, access, and the status shown in the app; it is not automatically available to every account. Review messages and usage limits before continuing.

## The Premium plan

Premium is one plan, paid monthly or yearly, bought in the Android app through Google Play once it is on sale. The price Google Play shows when you buy is the one that applies. The plan renews automatically until you cancel.

- **Buying:** sign in, open **Me → Premium & usage**, choose monthly or yearly, then confirm in Google Play.
- **Cancelling:** at any time in **Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions** or with **Manage plan**. Premium stays until the end of the period you paid for.
- **Refunds:** under Google Play's refund policy.
- **New phone or reinstall:** sign in to the same Nilaza account and choose **Restore purchases**. Premium is linked to your Nilaza account, not to one phone.
- **Deleting your account:** does not cancel the Google Play plan; cancel it first.

After buying, some AI features may take a few minutes to open. AI interpretation keeps reasonable usage limits. Details: [Terms of use](/en/terms) and [Privacy](/en/privacy).

Astrology is a symbolic lens for entertainment and self-reflection, not fixed destiny or professional advice.
