## What is the waitlist for?

The Nilaza website is for people interested in the app before launch. When the form is ready, you can sign up for launch news and early access opportunities. If a connection notice is shown, the website cannot accept a signup yet; please check back later.

## How to sign up

Enter your email, choose your interest, read Privacy and actively agree to updates. Complete spam verification and choose Join the waitlist. Only the recorded confirmation means your request was saved. If submission fails, your details are kept so you can verify again and retry.

Signing up again with the same email does not create another contact. Previously unsubscribed contacts are not automatically restored to the sending audience.

## What signup does not guarantee

Signup is not an app account, a download entitlement, an immediate invitation or a confirmed launch date. Nilaza is in internal testing; app captures do not establish store release. No payment is required to sign up.

## Email and consent

Your signup request is recorded first; confirmation and update email delivery is a separate step. The website does not promise an automatic email immediately after signup. Once a campaign is activated, updates will include a way to unsubscribe; unconfirmed or suppressed addresses are not considered eligible for sending.

Premium prices and benefits become commitments only when officially published. Do not provide passwords or recovery codes in the form.
