## Open a reading

Each draw shows the newly revealed card at the center with your draw count. For a multi-card spread, choose **Draw next card** to continue. Once all cards are revealed, choose **View message** to open the overall interpretation. Tap a revealed card for a closer look.

Choose a deck and spread, enter a question, then draw cards as guided. The app selects the cards; AI interprets only the selected cards and the context you provide.

## Ask better questions

Prefer open questions such as “What do I need to see more clearly?” or “What small step is within my control?”. Do not treat tarot as evidence for medical, legal, or financial decisions.

You can save a useful interpretation to your Journal for later reflection.
