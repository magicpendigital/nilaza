## Premium plan

Nilaza offers one Premium plan, paid monthly or yearly, bought in the Android app through Google Play.

- **Price:** the price and currency shown by Google Play when you buy are the ones that apply. This page does not list a fixed price, because prices can differ by country and over time.
- **Auto-renewal:** the plan renews automatically at the end of each period (month or year) and Google Play charges your payment method until you cancel.
- **Cancelling:** you can cancel at any time in **Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions**, or through **Manage plan** in the app. After cancelling, the plan stops renewing and Premium stays until the end of the period you paid for.
- **Refunds:** payments and refunds are handled by Google Play, under Google Play's refund policy and the law where you live. Nilaza does not take payments directly and does not issue refunds outside that process.
- **Linked to your account:** Premium is linked to the Nilaza account you were signed in with when you bought it. On a new phone, sign in to the same account and choose **Restore purchases**.
- **Deleting your account does not cancel the plan:** if you delete your Nilaza account, the Google Play subscription keeps running until you cancel it in Google Play. See [Delete your account and data](/en/account-deletion).
- **Benefits:** Premium benefits are what the app describes when you buy. AI features keep reasonable usage limits so the service stays stable. Tarot, Astrology, and AI content are prompts for reflection and do not guarantee any outcome.
