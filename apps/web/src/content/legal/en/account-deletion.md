## Delete in the app

Open **Me › Account › Delete account**, read the explanation and confirm twice. You need to be signed in. Deletion cannot be undone.

If you do not see this option yet, the app version you use has not turned on in-app deletion; use the method below.

## Delete without the app

Send a deletion request from the same email address you use to sign in to Nilaza to the support mailbox. The support mailbox is being set up and will be listed here before Nilaza opens testing. We only act on requests sent from the account's own email, so no one can delete someone else's account by mistake.

## What is deleted

- Your sign-in account.
- Your profile and birth profile.
- Synced journal entries, conversations with guides and the Council, and Tarot and Astrology readings saved on our servers.
- Technical logs of AI requests linked to your account, usage events and pending generation requests.
- A waitlist sign-up with the same email, if any.

Data kept only on your phone is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

## What is kept

- The outcome of safety-filter interventions is kept for abuse statistics, but **any description that may quote you is removed** and it is no longer linked to your account.
- A deletion record holding only a hash (no email or name), so an interrupted deletion can resume; it is removed 90 days after completion.

Accounts of Nilaza operations staff cannot be deleted this way; their access must be removed first.
