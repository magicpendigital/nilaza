## Delete in the app

Open **Me › Account › Delete account**, read the explanation and confirm twice. You need to be signed in. Deletion cannot be undone.

If you do not see this option yet, the app version you use has not turned on in-app deletion; use the method below.

## If you have Premium

Deleting your account **does not cancel** your Google Play subscription, and Google Play may keep charging you. Cancel it first in **Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions**. Once the account is deleted, the Premium access linked to it is gone too; any refund follows Google Play's policy.

## Delete without the app

Email the support mailbox with the subject **"Delete my Nilaza account"** from the same address you use to sign in to Nilaza: [magicpendigital@gmail.com](mailto:magicpendigital@gmail.com?subject=Delete%20my%20Nilaza%20account). We only act on requests sent from the account's own email, so no one can delete someone else's account by mistake.

## What is deleted

- Your sign-in account.
- Your profile and birth profile.
- Synced journal entries, conversations with guides and the Council, and Tarot and Astrology readings saved on our servers.
- Technical logs of AI requests linked to your account, usage events and pending generation requests.
- A waitlist sign-up with the same email, if any.
- Your Premium plan status on Nilaza's servers, and the customer record and purchase history held by our subscription-management provider (RevenueCat).

Data kept only on your phone is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

## What is kept

- The outcome of safety-filter interventions is kept for abuse statistics, but **any description that may quote you is removed** and it is no longer linked to your account.
- A deletion record holding only a hash (no email or name), so an interrupted deletion can resume; it is removed 90 days after completion.
- A technical log of payment notifications received (event ID, event type, time and the random user ID, with no email, name or price) so the same transaction is not applied twice; retention: **[OWNER TO DECIDE: retention period]**.
- Transaction records kept by Google Play under Google's policies; Nilaza cannot delete these.

Accounts of Nilaza operations staff cannot be deleted this way; their access must be removed first.
