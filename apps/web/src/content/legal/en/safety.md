## Reflection, not destiny

Tarot and astrology are used as symbolic language. Nilaza avoids claims that an event will certainly happen.

## Professional boundaries

Nilaza does not diagnose, prescribe, make legal or financial decisions, or act as a mental-health professional.

## When you are not safe

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency service or a trusted person nearby. Nilaza does not guess crisis contact numbers based on location.

## Face imagery

Face reading is not in the current release. If developed, it will be symbolic only, with explicit consent and deletion controls; it will not infer protected traits, health, intelligence, trustworthiness, or identity.
