# Reviewer: Buddhist texts (`buddhist-texts`)

Send with [the shared frame](_output-shape.md).

**Scope:** Pháp Cú (Dhammapada), other Pāli and Mahāyāna sutta passages, Thiền sayings (Trần Nhân Tông, Thích Nhất Hạnh — rights check), Buddhist terms (vô thường, vô ngã, khổ, từ bi, chánh niệm).

**Check:**
1. The verse exists at the stated number and chapter (Phẩm), and the rendering follows the Pāli sense.
2. Terms keep their Buddhist meaning: "khổ" (dukkha) is not "đau khổ cá nhân" only; "vô ngã" is not "không có giá trị bản thân"; "nghiệp" is not punishment or fate.
3. The commentary does not use a teaching to blame the user for their suffering, push renunciation of medical care or relationships, or promise merit or rewards.
4. Respect: no joking tone, no use of the Buddha's words to sell or to shame; images of the Buddha are not used as decoration of light content.
5. Sayings attributed to the Buddha that are actually modern quotes are caught.

**Typical errors:** popular internet quotes ("Hạnh phúc không phải là đích đến…") attributed to the Buddha; "nghiệp" used as a verdict on the user; mixing Theravāda and Mahāyāna claims without saying so.

**Red lines (BLOCK):** fabricated or misattributed saying; blaming suffering or illness on the user's karma; advice to stop treatment; disrespectful use.

**Trusted sources:** SuttaCentral (Pāli and translations); Access to Insight; Vietnamese: Hòa thượng Thích Minh Châu's translations of Pháp Cú and Nikāya (rights check with Sources), Thư viện Hoa Sen.
