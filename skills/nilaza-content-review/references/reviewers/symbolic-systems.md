# Reviewer: Symbolic systems (`symbolic-systems`)

Send with [the shared frame](_output-shape.md). Tarot card copy is not yours: it goes to `nilaza-tarot-content-qc`.

**Scope:** Western astrology (signs, planets, houses, aspects, transits, Moon phases), numerology / Bản đồ số (Pythagorean), Rune Elder Futhark (24 runes, three ættir), other symbol sets the app uses.

**Check:**
1. Symbol facts: rune names, order and ætt, sound value, shape; planet rulerships; sign element and modality; numerology reduction rules and master numbers. Any calculated claim must follow the app's calculated placements, not invented ones.
2. Meanings stay within the tradition's attested range; say when a meaning is modern (for example the blank rune "Wyrd" is a 1980s addition, merkstave / reversed meanings are modern).
3. The reading invites reflection ("điều này gợi bạn nghĩ về…"), never forecasts ("bạn sẽ gặp", "tháng này mất tiền").
4. Rune shapes and names avoid forms linked to extremist movements: Othala with wings / serifs, the "Wolfsangel", the doubled Sowilō "SS" form, the Algiz "Lebensrune" used as a political symbol. Plain Elder Futhark letter forms with a historical frame are fine.
5. Rune history is honest: runes are an alphabet first; divinatory use is thinly attested (Tacitus' "Germania" ch. 10 mentions lots, not runes by name).

**Typical errors:** Ralph Blum's modern meanings presented as ancient; astrology lines that state fixed fate; numerology "số xấu"; unattested "Viking" claims.

**Red lines (BLOCK):** extremist-associated form or framing; prediction of illness, death, money loss or a partner's actions; "bad" numbers or signs that label the user.

**Trusted sources:** Elder Futhark: Page "Runes" (1987), Rune poems (Old English, Norwegian, Icelandic — for the later Futhorc / Younger Futhark, note it); ADL Hate Symbols Database for extremist forms; astrology: the app's calculation engine output and classical rulership tables.
