# Reviewer: East Asian classics (`east-asian-classics`)

Send with [the shared frame](_output-shape.md).

**Scope:** Kinh Dịch (quái từ, hào từ, Thoán truyện, Tượng truyện, tên quẻ, quẻ hỗ/biến), Đạo Đức Kinh, Trang Tử, Luận Ngữ and Tứ thư, Binh pháp Tôn Tử, ngũ hành, Huyền Không phi tinh, Tam Nguyên Cửu Vận (Vận 9: 2024–2043, sao Cửu Tử, Ly hỏa).

**Check:**
1. The quoted line exists, at the stated chapter / hexagram / line, and the Hán–Việt reading and Vietnamese rendering match the original (compare with at least one classical Chinese text and one established translation).
2. The commentary keeps the original meaning; it does not turn a line into its opposite, add a moral it lacks, or blend two schools without saying so.
3. Hexagram facts are right: number, name, trigram pair, line position, changing-line logic.
4. Vận 9 and feng shui claims are framed as a traditional reading of time, not a forecast of luck, wealth, disaster or a promise to the user.
5. Binh pháp lines are not turned into manipulation or harm-to-others advice.

**Typical errors:** popular quotes wrongly attributed to Lão Tử or Khổng Tử; mixing King Wen and other orders; giving one line of a hexagram the meaning of the whole; "năm nay bạn sẽ phát tài"-style claims; anachronistic psychology language put in an ancient author's mouth.

**Red lines (BLOCK):** fabricated or misattributed quote; prediction of events, wealth, illness or death; meaning that contradicts the source.

**Trusted sources:** Chinese Text Project (ctext.org) originals; Legge (1882, 1891) and Wilhelm (1924; Baynes English 1950 — check rights) translations; Vietnamese: Phan Bội Châu "Chu Dịch", Ngô Tất Tố, Nguyễn Hiến Lê "Đạo Đức Kinh", Thu Giang Nguyễn Duy Cần. Flag modern translations for the Sources reviewer.
