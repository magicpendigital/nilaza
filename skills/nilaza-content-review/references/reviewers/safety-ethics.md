# Reviewer: Safety and ethics (`safety-ethics`)

Send with [the shared frame](_output-shape.md).

**Scope:** every item in every batch.

**Check:**
1. **Reflect, not predict:** no forecast of events, health, money, exams, a partner's feelings or actions, death; no "định mệnh", "số phận đã an bài", "chắc chắn".
2. **No fatalism or fear:** no "xui", "đại hạn", "số xấu", curses, threats, guilt; hard themes are offered as questions with a way forward.
3. **No professional advice:** no medical, mental-health treatment, financial, investment or legal instructions; suggest seeing a professional when the topic touches these.
4. **Crisis:** content touching self-harm, suicide or violence carries a gentle frame and, where the item is user-facing in a risky moment, points to 115 only (not other hotlines).
5. **Respect and fairness:** no claims based on protected traits (religion, ethnicity, gender, sexuality, disability, age); no shaming of bodies, traits or choices; no content that encourages controlling or harming others.
6. **Store and youth safety:** nothing sexual, gambling-like or that encourages paying to change one's fate.
7. **Privacy:** content does not ask users to share sensitive data beyond the feature's need.

**Typical errors:** "Hôm nay không nên ký hợp đồng"; "quẻ này báo hiệu chia tay"; a Kiều line about death drawn for a sad user without a frame.

**Red lines (BLOCK):** any prediction or fatal verdict; self-harm or violence content without a safe frame; professional advice; content targeting protected traits; extremist content.

**Trusted sources:** `docs/BRAND_GUIDELINES.md` (reflective boundary), `AGENTS.md`, Google Play policy on deceptive and harmful content.
