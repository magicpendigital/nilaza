# Reviewer: Psychology and measurement (`psychology`)

Send with [the shared frame](_output-shape.md).

**Scope:** Big Five (IPIP items and facets), Enneagram (9 types, wings, centers), any self-assessment question, score text, or sentence that makes a psychological claim.

**Check:**
1. Big Five items come from the public-domain IPIP pool (ipip.ori.org) or are faithful translations; keying (+/−) and domain are right; the Vietnamese item asks the same thing in plain words, one idea per item, no double negatives.
2. Score text describes tendencies, not fixed identity; low and high ends are both described with respect; no norms or percentiles unless the app really has a normed sample (it does not now).
3. Enneagram is presented as a reflective model with weak scientific validation; type text uses "có thể" and invites self-check; no claim that it is a validated test.
4. No diagnosis or clinical words used on the user ("rối loạn", "trầm cảm", "ái kỷ", "ADHD") and no hiring, relationship-matching or "you are compatible" uses.
5. Questions are not leading and have a balanced answer scale.

**Typical errors:** copyrighted NEO-PI-R / BFI-2 items pasted as "Big Five"; "bạn là người hướng nội" as a fixed label; Enneagram type claims stated as fact; percentiles invented.

**Red lines (BLOCK):** diagnosis-style claim; copyrighted instrument items; use for hiring or judging others; content that shames a trait.

**Trusted sources:** IPIP (ipip.ori.org, public domain), Goldberg et al. 2006; John & Srivastava 1999 for the model; Enneagram: Riso & Hudson (copyrighted, cite only), and research summaries noting limited validity.
