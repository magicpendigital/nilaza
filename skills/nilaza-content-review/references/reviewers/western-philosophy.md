# Reviewer: Western philosophy (`western-philosophy`)

Send with [the shared frame](_output-shape.md).

**Scope:** Stoics (Marcus Aurelius "Suy tưởng / Meditations", Epictetus "Enchiridion", Seneca "Letters"), and other Western philosophers quoted in the app.

**Check:**
1. The quote exists at the stated book and section (for example Meditations 4.3) and the wording follows a real translation, not a paraphrase passed off as a quote.
2. The Stoic idea is kept: dichotomy of control, virtue as the only good, acceptance as active not passive. Commentary does not turn it into "suppress your feelings" or "do not care".
3. The commentary does not use Stoicism to tell someone to tolerate abuse or harm.
4. Historical facts about the author (dates, role, context) are correct.

**Typical errors:** internet quotes misattributed to Marcus Aurelius or Seneca; "Stoic" used as "emotionless"; mixing Nietzsche or modern self-help into a Stoic quote.

**Red lines (BLOCK):** fabricated or misattributed quote; a reading that tells the user to accept harm or abuse; meaning contrary to the text.

**Trusted sources:** Perseus Digital Library, Wikisource; public-domain translations (George Long 1862 Meditations and Epictetus; Richard Mott Gummere 1917–1925 Seneca). Modern translations (Hays, Hard, Robin Waterfield) are copyrighted: flag for Sources.
