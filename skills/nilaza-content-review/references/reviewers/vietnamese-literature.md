# Reviewer: Vietnamese literature (`vietnamese-literature`)

Send with [the shared frame](_output-shape.md).

**Scope:** Truyện Kiều (Nguyễn Du, 3254 câu lục bát), the Bói Kiều tradition, ca dao, tục ngữ, thơ Việt quoted in the app.

**Check:**
1. Each Kiều line matches the stated line number and an established edition; note edition differences (Đào Duy Anh, Bùi Kỷ – Trần Trọng Kim, Nguyễn Thạch Giang) instead of calling them errors.
2. A Bói Kiều selection is a coherent couplet (câu 6 + câu 8) and its reading fits the passage's real context in the story (who speaks, what happens).
3. Commentary respects the traditional Bói Kiều spirit (a mirror for reflection) and does not present it as a forecast.
4. Passages about rape, being sold, suicide (Kiều trầm mình ở sông Tiền Đường), violence or humiliation are excluded from a random draw pool unless the reading carries a safe, reflective frame; say which line numbers to exclude.
5. Explanations of Hán–Việt words and điển cố are right.
6. Ca dao and tục ngữ have no single author: never attribute them to a person.

**Typical errors:** wrong line numbers; a câu 6 paired with the wrong câu 8; reading a line by its surface words while the story context means the opposite; mixing up characters (Thúy Kiều / Thúy Vân, Kim Trọng / Thúc Sinh / Từ Hải).

**Red lines (BLOCK):** misquoted or invented line; a reading that predicts misfortune, death or a partner's betrayal; a trauma passage in the random pool without a safe frame.

**Trusted sources:** Đào Duy Anh edition (1979), Nguyễn Thạch Giang (1972), Thư viện Viện Hán Nôm (nom.org.vn / Truyện Kiều Nôm texts), Wikisource Truyện Kiều. Truyện Kiều is in the public domain; modern annotations are not (flag for Sources).
