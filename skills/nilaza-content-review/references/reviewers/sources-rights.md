# Reviewer: Sources and rights (`sources-rights`)

Send with [the shared frame](_output-shape.md).

**Scope:** every item in every batch.

**Check:**
1. Each quote names work, location (chapter, verse, line, section) and the translation used; Nilaza's own translation is labelled as such ("Nilaza dịch").
2. Rights: the original text and the translation used are public domain, openly licensed, or Nilaza's own. Vietnam: works are generally protected for the author's life + 50 years; US / EU commonly life + 70 — when a translator's death date is unknown or within those terms, treat the translation as protected.
3. Protected translations or commentary: at most a short attributed quote, not as a daily-content pool; otherwise ask for a Nilaza translation from a public-domain original.
4. Modern authors (Thích Nhất Hạnh, Riso & Hudson, Ralph Blum, NEO-PI-R) are cited only, not copied.
5. Commentary is original Nilaza writing, not a paraphrase of a copyrighted book.
6. Attribution is not inflated: an anonymous saying is not given an author.

**Typical errors:** "Đạo Đức Kinh" lines copied from a recent paid translation; Thích Minh Châu text without a rights note; IPIP-looking items that are actually from a copyrighted test.

**Red lines (BLOCK):** text Nilaza has no right to use at this length; misattribution.

**Output extra:** in `evidence`, state the rights basis (`public-domain: <reason>`, `nilaza-translation`, `licensed: <licence>`, or `unknown`). `unknown` means `UNSURE`, never `PASS`.

**Trusted sources:** Wikisource / Project Gutenberg rights notes, ctext.org, SuttaCentral licence pages, ipip.ori.org, the translator's dates from library catalogues (Thư viện Quốc gia Việt Nam, WorldCat).
